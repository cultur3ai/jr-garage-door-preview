What we fix

Six things that go wrong, and what each one actually is.

01

Broken spring

The bang from the garage was the torsion spring letting go. The door is a dead weight now — don’t force it.

02

Opener runs, door doesn’t move

Motor spinning with nothing lifting usually means a stripped gear, a tired capacitor, or a spring no longer carrying the weight.

03

Door off the track

A bent track or a roller out of its channel has to be straightened before it chews the panels.

04

Cables and rollers

Frayed cables and flat-spotted rollers are how a small repair turns into a dropped door.

05

New door installation

Measured, supplied and fitted — plus the opener to match.

06

Panels and hardware

A dented section, a bent hinge, a handle that no longer latches.
